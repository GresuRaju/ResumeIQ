// The Approach

Why Every Score
is Explainable.

Similarity
TF-IDF Cosine Distance

scikit-learn vectorizes both the resume and JD into TF-IDF space. Cosine angle between the two vectors gives a 0→1 score that reflects true semantic overlap, not just exact keyword hits.

vect.fit_transform([doc_a, doc_b])
cosine_similarity(tfidf[0], tfidf[1])
Skills
Regex + Vocabulary Matching

An 80-skill vocabulary covers programming, ML/DL frameworks, NLP, data, MLOps, cloud, and soft skills. Word-boundary regex prevents false matches. Multi-word phrases matched as substrings.

re.search(r'\b' + skill + r'\b',
text_lower)
ATS Logic
6 Deterministic Checks

Each ATS criterion is a real-world hiring filter: keyword density, years of experience, contact presence, education detection, section structure, and document length. Every check is shown to the user.

pass_count / len(criteria) × 60
+ keyword_ratio × 40 → ats_score
